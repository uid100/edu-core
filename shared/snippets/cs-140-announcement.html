Opportunity: CIS 140 Databases (3 Units, 8 Weeks)

Hello Everyone,

I wanted to make you aware of a great opportunity available this semester. CIS 140: Databases is currently being offered in an 8-week short-term format, allowing students to earn 3 units in a compressed schedule.

Database skills are valuable for both Computer Science (CS) and Computer Information Systems (CIS) students. Whether your interests include software development, cybersecurity, networking, systems administration, data analytics, or information systems, understanding how data is organized, stored, retrieved, and managed is an important technical skill.

The course covers fundamental database concepts including data modeling, tables, relationships, keys, queries, forms, reports, normalization, and database design. These concepts appear throughout many technical disciplines and are commonly used in industry.

The course currently has relatively low enrollment, which can provide students with additional opportunities for interaction, feedback, and support throughout the semester.

If you are looking for an additional course that complements your CS or CIS studies while earning units in a short-term format, consider taking a look at CIS 140.
